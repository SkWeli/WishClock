    What everything costs

    Every item, with today’s price and the price on the day you buy it. Open a row to see what’s inside.

    Each month the plan adds everyone’s pay (an intern’s allowance until they go permanent, then yearly raises), takes away living costs (rising with inflation), loan instalments and running costs, and adds interest on savings. When the people paying for the next goal have enough saved for that month’s price, they buy it.
